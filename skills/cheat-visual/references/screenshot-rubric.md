# Screenshot Rubric

Score candidates from 0-2 on each item. Prefer candidates with total score >= 8.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source quality | unknown / repost | secondary source | official or primary source |
| Article relevance | decorative | loosely related | supports a specific paragraph or claim |
| Context preservation | cropped too hard | partial context | source identity and key context visible |
| Freshness | stale or unclear | acceptable | clearly current for the topic |
| Visual clarity | hard to read | usable | readable on mobile |
| Useful crop | full-page or noisy | some excess context | useless areas removed and key info enlarged |
| Misleading risk | high | manageable | low |

## Hard rejects

Reject candidates when:
- the content is a screenshot of a screenshot and the original is findable;
- the source identity is absent;
- the claim is not visible in the screenshot;
- the content is mostly decorative;
- the image is an agent-made UI card instead of source-backed evidence;
- the screenshot would likely mislead readers about what the source proves;
- the content is behind a login/paywall and cannot be fairly shown.

## Mobile readability

WeChat readers often view on phones. Prefer tight crops, large text, and simple framing. Avoid dense full-page screenshots unless the page layout itself matters.

For charts and tables, preserve labels and the row/axis that makes the claim interpretable. Crop the rest.
